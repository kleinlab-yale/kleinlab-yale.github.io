TAYLOR TOWN LIFE — THIRD-PARTY ASSETS

The game code and interface are original to this project. The following 3D
model packs are distributed under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0) and are
bundled locally so the game makes no runtime API calls.

KayKit Character Pack: Adventurers
https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Character-Pack-Adventures-1.0
Used for the animated player base model.

Kenney Cube Pets
https://kenney.nl/assets/cube-pets
Used for the cat, dog, and bunny companion models.

Kenney City Kit: Suburban
https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-suburban
Used for village building models.

Kenney Nature Kit
https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit
Used for trees, flowers, grasses, bushes, mushrooms, and stones.

Kenney Fantasy Town Kit
https://kenney.nl/assets/fantasy-town-kit
Used for village props including the fountain, market stall, lanterns, cart,
fence, and hedges.

Kenney Furniture Kit
https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit
Used for collectible and placeable cottage furniture.

Copies of each source pack's license are included in the licenses directory.

The welcome-screen artwork and television channel artwork in public/art were
generated specifically for Taylor Town Life and are not third-party assets.
