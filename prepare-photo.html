Prepare a photo for the website

Crop, adjust, and save a smaller JPEG that’s ready to upload in Pages CMS. Your photo is processed here in your browser.

1. Choose your photo

Your original photo stays unchanged.

Choose a photo to get started.

2. Crop and adjust

Drag the photo to reposition it, or use the position sliders. Zoom in to crop more closely.

Save your edited copy

In the save options, choose Save Image or Save to Photos. For a file stored on your phone, choose Save to Files → On My iPhone.

Photos follows your phone’s existing iCloud Photos setting. Choose On My iPhone for a device-only file.

Touch and hold this edited image, then choose Save to Photos or Save Image.

Download a JPEG instead

To keep Safari downloads on your phone, first set Settings → Apps → Safari → Downloads → On My iPhone. The download button uses your browser’s chosen location.

Download JPEG

3. Add it to the website

Open Lab photos in the editor and tap Add an item. Upload the edited JPEG from Photos or On My iPhone, enter your caption and image description, then tap Save.
