What the model encodes

The model follows the logic in your uploaded schematics. Site 1 binding by NELL2 clusters ROS1 ectodomains but does not activate them. Full activation is shown only after the arm peels away, the leg becomes more dynamic, and the receptors collapse toward a common axis near the membrane.

Structural anchors

This is a mechanistic cartoon, not an atom-by-atom simulation. It is grounded in the structures reported in the paper and linked PDB entries: 9PVP, 10FT, 10GH, 9DZ4, and 9PWQ.

How to use it

Open this page in a browser, rotate the scene, and switch between the three states. If you want the next step, I can turn this into a real atomic viewer that loads the PDB structures directly or export the schematic as a Blender-friendly mesh.
