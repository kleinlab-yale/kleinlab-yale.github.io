RTK Signal Fate: Density-Dependent Kinetics

High density (R >> Kd) enables rapid dimerization but accelerates ligand depletion.

α > 1 (Negative): Buffers signal at low density.
α < 1 (Positive): Sharpens the burst.

Observation: High R₀ with Pos Coop creates a high-amplitude, short-duration "Burst". High α with low R₀ creates a sustained "Plateau".
