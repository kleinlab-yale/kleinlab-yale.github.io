Primary knobs

Cooperativity regime

Negative cooperativity is represented as a broader, weaker dimerization response; positive cooperativity is sharper and stronger.

Trafficking

Strong activation drives receptor loss from the surface, which shortens the pulse.

Model structure

The simulator is now tuned as a teaching model for the curve shapes you asked for. It uses three linked ideas:

  1. Receptor abundance, ligand, cooperativity, and Kd set an effective dimerization drive that decides whether the response is mostly transient or mostly sustained after a fixed 1 minute pulse at time zero.
  2. A fast transient component peaks near 2 minutes, while a weak negative-cooperative regime adds a low plateau that can persist for 30 to 60 minutes.
  3. Strong signaling drives rapid receptor loss from the surface, which makes transient responses crash even if ligand is still present.
