    Teaching notes

    • Tabs 1 and 2 are normalized to fractions: binding uses `[LR]/[R0]` and efficacy uses `E/Emax`.
    • The antagonist classification tab links mechanism families to the expected shape changes on dose-response curves.
    • Concentration sliders use linear nM controls from 0.1 to 250 while the plotted x-axes stay fixed for comparison.
    • Linear concentration plots cap at 1000 nM. Quantal dose uses mg/kg on a log x-axis from 1 to 200.
