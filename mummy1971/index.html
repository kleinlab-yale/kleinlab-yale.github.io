Faithfully recreated from the original 1971 Milton Bradley Voice of the Mummy Directions (4165) and dual-sided 40-groove internal phonograph record.

📄 Open Original 1971 Rulebook PDF

💿 PART I: Side 1 of Record (Hunt for Jewels)

  • Setup: 26 Emerald Scarab Jewels (1 pt each) sit on the Winged Scarab spaces (12 on Level 1, 8 on Level 2, 6 on Level 3). The Great Jewel (+5 pts) and Ruby Cobra sit on the Sarcophagus.
  • Movement: All Explorers start on their Home Lotus Corner on Level 1 and move CLOCKWISE by the count rolled on the die. All corner sections count as a space.
  • Collecting Jewels: Ending your move on a space with a Scarab Jewel collects it into your Temple. Ending on an opponent's space steals 1 Jewel from each opponent there!
  • Mummy's Head Spaces: Landing on a Pharaoh Mask requires pulling the Sarcophagus Lever to hear and obey a random Side A voice command!
  • Changing Levels (Rule 7): Ending your turn on any Corner space or Protruding Staircase space lets you climb UP or DOWN 1 level for your next turn.
  • End of Part I (Rules 8 & 9): Once all 6 Top-Level Jewels on Level 3 are collected, the first Explorer to land by EXACT COUNT on either Level 3 Great Jewel Space collects both the Great Jewel (+5 pts) and the cursed Ruby Cobra (-5 pts Evil Spell)!

💿 PART II: Side 2 of Record (Escape to Your Temple)

  • Record Flip: The Sarcophagus record flips to Side #2 (Side B). Explorers may now move CLOCKWISE or COUNTER-CLOCKWISE each turn!
  • Passing the Cursed Cobra (Rule II.2): While holding the Cobra, you cannot end the game. Landing by EXACT COUNT on an opponent steals 1 Jewel AND passes the Cobra and its Evil Spell to that opponent!
  • Stealing the Great Jewel (Rule II.3): Landing by EXACT COUNT on the opponent holding the Great Jewel steals the Great Jewel from them (without taking the Cobra!).
  • Ending the Game & Homecoming Bonus (Rules II.6 & II.7): The first Explorer without the Cobra to reach their own Level 1 Home Lotus Corner by EXACT COUNT ends the game and receives 1 bonus Jewel from each opponent (or the Great Jewel if an opponent holds only the Great Jewel)!
  • Winning: 1 pt per Precious Jewel, +5 pts for the Great Jewel, -5 pts for the Cobra. Highest score wins!
