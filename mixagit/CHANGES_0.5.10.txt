MIXA 0.5.10-preview: Local inference and performance update

Calibrated uncertainty
- Added experiment-specific parametric calibration of nuisance-refitted fit statistics.
- Supports physical parameters and exact model-specific KD equality constraints.
- Critical values come from the current fitted experiment, not benchmark tuning.
- Finite median baselines, shared controls and enabled concentration budgets are simulated.
- Reports critical-value Monte Carlo precision, all refits, failures and search limits.
- Open/incomplete limits and better-optimum cases remain unresolved.
- A fresh 300-experiment KD study returned 300 intervals: 93%, 94%, 94% coverage
  for nominal 95% intervals, with Monte Carlo uncertainty explicitly retained.

Complex-model performance
- Exact sequential two-analyte propagation when no transport requires an ODE.
- LSODA for nonlinear avidity/transport, with analytic state Jacobians.
- Same reaction equations, tolerances, physical phase history and observation selections.
- Retained Radau comparison path and independent reaction-network reference checks.
- All 30 three-start blind searches completed within the same 30-second budget;
  the previous study completed 15/30. Prediction is not parameter identifiability.

Joint repeat/reference/calibration inference
- Added a separate Bayesian nonlinear 1:1 analysis for at least three independent repeats.
- Jointly estimates correlated repeat log-rates, linked capacities and population variation.
- Shared raw reference covariance and concentration factors are common across their actual uses.
- A common uncertain stock does not average away with more repeats.
- Undoes median subtraction and explicitly models measured baselines in the response likelihood.
- Analytically integrates Gaussian baseline/correction terms and population log-rate means.
- Explicit editable proper priors; no hidden score-based prior selection.
- Analytic-gradient Metropolis-adjusted Langevin sampling, exact conditional stock updates,
  slice hyperparameter sampling and prior-preserving centered/noncentered interweaving.
- Four independent complete chains, rank/folded split Rhat and bulk/tail ESS gates.
- Analytic augmented-Jacobian SVD and exact shared concentration-shift updates
  address the identified very-low-noise calibration ridge.
- Separates population credible intervals, repeat estimates, model-based between-repeat
  spread, mean-curve uncertainty and new-repeat parameter/measurement prediction.
- Retains initial slow-mixing studies, all incomplete chains and exploratory outputs.
- Supports saved prior-sensitivity runs without overwriting the original fit.
- All joint nuisance gates passed in 10 fixed-population and 12 prior-generated
  datasets across 88 complete independent chains; raw coverage/ranks remain reported.

Preservation and traceability
- Existing manual/assisted analysis, equilibrium, diagnostic panels and repeat mean +/- SD
  publication outputs remain. No frontend redesign or automatic data exclusion.
- New figures, curve source data, draws, diagnostics, priors, equations and primary sources
  are downloadable with the analysis, standalone review and documentation packages.
- No new dependencies, hardware/billing changes, or live Hugging Face upload.
- Removed duplicate widget-default warnings from persistent input-budget controls.

Supported scope
- Joint inference is initially 1:1, not a hierarchy for all advanced mechanisms.
- Raw-source SD ratios and OU times are still declared/plug-in inputs.
- Arbitrary dilution covariance and phase/model-selection uncertainty are not integrated.
- Finite synthetic validation is reported as such, not universal calibration certification.
