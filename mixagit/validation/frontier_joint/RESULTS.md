# MIXA Joint Validation

Prespecified nonlinear response-level 1:1 hierarchical studies with 3/6 independent repeats, reused zero control, 10% common stock and 3% shared concentration-level uncertainty. Population parameters are fixed truth; random repeat rates are generated independently. Small outer counts assess recovery, computational diagnostics and interval availability; they are not nominal-coverage certification. Fixed source SD ratios and OU times, priors, seeds and every incomplete chain are retained. No fitted-rate averages are used as likelihood observations.
